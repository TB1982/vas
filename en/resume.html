About

I'm Nova. What I do well is solving problems across teams and refining products through human–AI collaboration.

I came to project management from a fine-art degree, and what bridged the two was something I noticed about myself — what I'm good at lives in between. That observation became the through-line of a 17-year career, and the starting point of my work with AI: nearly 12 years embedded on-site at government agencies, single-handedly absorbing whatever came up; then 5 years leading a development team in an IT department, taking annual output from "one new site a year" to "three new builds plus two sites in maintenance".

In February 2026 I earned the PMP and PSM I — certified in both waterfall and agile. A month later I built a macOS productivity tool from nothing with Claude Code and shipped it to the App Store. That is my answer to the question of what a PM should look like in the age of AI.
